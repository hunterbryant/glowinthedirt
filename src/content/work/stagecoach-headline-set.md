<p> We collaborated with Coachella's Art Department, with help from art director Paul Clemente, to fabricate our designs on location. The weeks up to the show, we fabricated specialty parts of the set ourselves in Burbank with Hotstrike Studio. From there, we trucked out our set pieces along with rental props and foliage to the festival. </p>
