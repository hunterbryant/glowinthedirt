Originally used as a studio for light & space artist Norman Zammitt, the unit’s massive murals and industrial windows make it a creative & welcoming environment.
Natural Light
Our large central skylight washes the room in natural light. We have built-in flexibility to quickly black out the skylight for easy lighting control. With the use of a haze or smoke machine (which we can provide), the skylight casts a heavenly beam.
Historic Murals
Two massive murals set a creative tone for the studio. This historically protected masterpiece, Elysium, is the work of light and space artist Norman Zammitt.
Industrial Windows
A massive 44' stretch of warehouse windows blocked from natural light can be artificially lit, allowing you to mimic various lighting conditions.
Dome Set
We offer a 10-meter diameter 'Dome' set. This bright inflatable set provides an enclosed blank slate for any creative project.
